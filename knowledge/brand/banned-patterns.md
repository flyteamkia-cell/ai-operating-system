# Banned patterns

Hard constraints on every generated artifact. The drafting prompt is evaluated against this file; a violation fails the output, it does not warn.

## Compliance (financial content) — to be completed, see [N5]
- No guaranteed-return or profit-promise phrasing.
- No signal-like instruction that could be read as personalized investment advice.
- Required disclaimer placement: TBD.

## Voice
- No generic engagement-bait openings.
- No hype register the operator would never say aloud.
- No borrowed authority ("experts say") without a named source.

## Structural
- No hook longer than TBD seconds.
- No CTA stacking (one ask per piece).
